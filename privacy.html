Privacy Policy

Last updated October 23, 2026

Stash is a free browser extension that helps you save and organize YouTube videos into personal lists. This policy explains what the extension accesses and where your information is stored.

Information Stash uses

When you choose to save a video, Stash reads information about the currently open YouTube video, such as its title, link, thumbnail, and channel name. Stash stores this information with your custom list names and saved-video selections so it can display your lists later.

Storage and sharing

Your lists and saved-video information are stored locally in your browser using Chrome's local extension storage. Stash does not require an account, does not operate a cloud sync service, and does not send your saved lists to the extension developer. Stash does not sell your information or use it for advertising or profiling.

Browser permissions

Stash uses Chrome permissions to save and retrieve lists and to identify the YouTube video in your active tab when you use the extension. Its website access is limited to YouTube pages needed for that feature.

Third-party services

Opening saved videos takes you to YouTube, which has its own privacy practices. The Stash promotional website may load fonts from Google Fonts; requests for those fonts are handled by Google. The extension does not run third-party analytics or advertisements.

Deleting information

You can remove individual saved videos or delete lists in the extension. Uninstalling Stash removes its locally stored extension data in the normal course of Chrome's extension removal process.

Changes

If Stash's data practices change, this page will be updated. Check the date at the top for the latest version.

Questions

For questions, please open an issue on the Stash website GitHub repository.
